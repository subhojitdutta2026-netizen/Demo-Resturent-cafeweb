## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, Michelin-star hospitality visual narrative designed for luxury culinary spaces. The aesthetic balances deep gastronomic mystery with luminous metallic warmth, targeted at discerning diners, culinary connoisseurs, and hospitality operators seeking elevated prestige.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep obsidian, tobacco, and espresso foundations create a nocturnal, candlelit ambiance, ensuring high contrast with culinary presentation.
- **Tailored Tactility:** Frosted glass panels paired with hairline champagne gold borders mirror vintage crystal glassware and brass architecture.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise geometric sans-serif body copy deliver the tone of a limited-edition tasting journal or private club digest.
- **Subtle Organic Resonance:** Rare, deliberate flashes of deep reserve emerald inject botanical vitality into an otherwise mineral and metallic environment.

## Colors

The palette operates in a calibrated low-key spectrum to simulate candlelit private dining rooms and brushed metal detailing.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Champagne Gold):** Used for focal CTA actions, fine keylines, iconography, active selection states, and premium badge monograms.
- **Secondary (`#1A1512` / Espresso Noir):** Serves as secondary surface layering, deep card fills, table headers, and structural partitions.
- **Tertiary (`#1B3B2B` / Reserve Emerald):** An understated accent reserved for sommelier badges, seasonal farm-to-table provenance marks, confirmation states, and organic wine designations.
- **Neutral (`#0C0C0E` / Deep Obsidian Charcoal):** The primary canvas background, creating limitless depth without the sterile harshness of absolute `#000000`.
- **Text & Foreground Warmth:** Editorial copy uses Warm Ivory (`#F7F4EB`) for maximum clarity and Champagne Mist (`#E3DEC3`) for secondary descriptions and metadata.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate dialogue between grand European gastronomic publishing and contemporary digital agility.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display` with deliberate optical spacing. Hero headings evoke bespoke printed menus and wine folios. Italic variants are reserved for sommelier notes, course titles, or regional provenance annotations.
- **Body & Functional Labels:** Handled by `Plus Jakarta Sans` in refined weights (300 to 500) to keep ingredient pairings, tasting menus, and operational details crisp and legible under ambient room conditions.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Styled with `label-caps` in uppercase format with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`) for categorical tags like "CHEF'S TABLE", "VINTAGE RESERVES", and "PRIVATE SALON".

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes expansive architectural negative space to mirror unhurried fine dining pacing.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout framed by generous canvas margins (`4rem`) and wide gutters (`2.5rem`). Content spans structured pairings (e.g., 5-column editorial text beside 7-column sensory food photography).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters, collapsing tertiary decorative sidebars into modal drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1.25rem margins and 1rem gutters. Sticky booking action bars fix to the bottom viewport with safe-area spacing.

### Vertical Rhythm
Vertical progression utilizes alternating compact groupings (`space-sm`, `space-md` for ingredient and pairing sets) and deliberate breathing pauses (`space-xl` and higher) between courses and narrative chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation conveys the atmospheric mystery of smoked glass partition walls and intimate spotlighting.

### Tonal Stratification & Glassmorphism
1. **Base Layer (Ground Zero):** Obsidian Black (`#0C0C0E`), static, deep, absorbing light.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Ambient Cards):** Espresso Noir (`#1A1512`) at 85% opacity with a subtle 16px backdrop blur, encased in a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Floating Modals & Menus):** Rich obsidian glass (`rgba(12, 12, 14, 0.72)`) backed by a 24px backdrop blur, reinforced with an inner top highlight of `rgba(247, 244, 235, 0.08)` and outer border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.

### Shadow Signature
Shadows are never harsh gray; they are cast as diffused umber-tinted halos (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` to `rgba(26, 21, 18, 0.45)`) extending 32px with an 8px spread, creating a float effect akin to a spotlight suspended over a VIP table.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, tailored geometry (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and content frames carry subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) corners, preserving a sharp, architectural, high-couture silhouette.
- Elevated reservation containers, dish detail cards, and modal sheets scale to 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Pills and circles are strictly isolated to circular badge counts, floating tasting tags, or audio ambiance controllers to avoid a playful or casual consumer-app aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Reservation CTA:** Rich champagne gold background (`#D4AF37`) with obsidian text (`#0C0C0E`), 4px border radius, uppercase medium tracking, and a smooth hover transition that adds a diffuse gold outer glow (`0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Wine List Inquiries:** Ghost treatment featuring a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, warm ivory text (`#F7F4EB`), and subtle background shift to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` upon hover.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- Miniature tags displaying terroir indicators, organic certifications, or dietary notes.
- Structured with an ultra-thin 1px border (`rgba(227, 222, 195, 0.2)`), translucent espresso fill (`#1A1512`), and 11px uppercase typography.
- Michelin/Sommelier highlights employ the subtle tertiary emerald accent (`#1B3B2B`) with soft gold typography.

### Menu Lists & Tasting Sequences
- Horizontal menu items feature high-contrast dish names in `Playfair Display`, followed by a delicate dotted gold leader rule connecting seamlessly to the right-aligned price.
- Sub-item components present tasting notes in `body-sm` warm champagne mist with pairing suggestions tagged below in muted gold text.

### Form Inputs (Reservations & Concierge Requests)
- Background fill of translucent obsidian (`rgba(26, 21, 18, 0.6)`) with an inactive baseline border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- On focus, the field activates a 1px solid champagne gold outline and a gentle internal gold glow, without harsh box-shadow artifacts.
- Dropdown menus deploy backdrop blur with gold dividers between dining time slots.

### Reservation & Table Experience Cards
- Cards feature layered glassmorphism over dimly lit, high-resolution interior photography.
- Encased in hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`), cards shift their border brightness to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` upon hover, accompanied by a slight upward translate of 2px.

### VIP Concierge Floating Bar
- A persistent bottom element for mobile and tablet containing quick party-size selectors, date pickers, and a primary booking button, styled as a dark frosted pill with subtle metallic rim lighting.
